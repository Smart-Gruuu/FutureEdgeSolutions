Fintechs often treat compliance as a bottleneck. Done right, it becomes a **competitive advantage**: faster go-to-market, fewer surprises, and stronger trust with partners and regulators.

## Start with licensing and scope

Know exactly what you're doing and where. Payment services, lending, e-money, and investment advice each have different rules. Map your activities to licenses and regulatory requirements *before* you scale into new products or geographies.

## AML and KYC

Design controls that scale. Automate where possible (screening, transaction monitoring) but keep clear ownership and review. Document your risk assessment, policies, and procedures. Regulators expect evidence of a **risk-based approach**, not a tick-the-box checklist.

## Operational resilience

Outages and cyber incidents are under the spotlight. Plan for failures: backup systems, incident response, and communication. Test regularly. Many jurisdictions now have explicit resilience and DORA-style requirements.

## Build a culture of compliance

Compliance is not just a team—it's everyone. Train product, engineering, and ops. Embed compliance in design and launch processes. Escalate early; fix issues before they become findings.

- You're entering a new jurisdiction or product set.
- You're preparing for a license application or audit.
- You've had findings and need to remediate and harden.
