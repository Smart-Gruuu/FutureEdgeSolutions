The healthcare industry continues to evolve rapidly. Here are the trends we're watching and how they affect strategy and operations.

## AI and automation

AI is moving from pilots to production. Use cases range from clinical decision support and documentation to revenue cycle and prior auth. The focus in 2024 is on **responsible deployment**: governance, fairness, and clear ownership.

## Interoperability and FHIR

FHIR and related regulations are pushing interoperability forward. Organizations that invest in structured data, APIs, and clear data governance will be better positioned for value-based care and partnerships.

## Value-based care and risk

Value-based arrangements are expanding. Success depends on data, analytics, care model redesign, and alignment across providers and payers. Many organizations are still building the foundations.

## What this means for you

- **Providers**: Prioritise data quality, EHR optimisation, and care model changes that support value-based contracts.
- **Payers**: Double down on member experience, utilisation management, and partner ecosystem.
- **Digital health**: Ensure clear evidence, integration paths, and compliance—especially in regulated markets.
